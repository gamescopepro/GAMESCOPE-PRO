GAMESCOPE PRO — Frequently Asked Questions

What is GAMESCOPE PRO?

GAMESCOPE PRO is an independent global video game catalog for discovering and searching games across PlayStation, PC and Xbox.

Which platforms are included?

The public catalog includes games with at least one supported version on PlayStation, PC or Xbox. A game is not excluded just because it also has a mobile version; mobile-only games are excluded.

Can I search for a game that is not shown in the current list?

Yes. Use the online game search on the homepage to search the catalog by a game's name. Search results depend on the upstream catalog and may not include every game ever released.

Are upcoming release dates guaranteed?

No. Release dates and platform information can change, and some entries may have estimated or incomplete details. Check the publisher or official store for the latest confirmed information before making a purchase or planning around a release.

How often is the catalog refreshed?

The online listing refreshes periodically while the site is open. The server checks RAWG, Steam featured listings and up to 16 queued Steam product details every 15 minutes. The broad SteamSpy catalog scan advances one bounded page per 24 hours; incomplete new Steam records are placed in the detail queue. The manual source-check button runs an on-demand RAWG check. Providers are queried within rate and page limits, so this does not mean every game in every store is checked on every cycle.

Is GAMESCOPE PRO connected to PlayStation Network or other gaming accounts?

The catalog is not a PlayStation Network client and does not connect to a user's PSN account. Account and library features provided by the website are separate from official platform accounts.

Where does game information come from?

The catalog combines RAWG with Steam discovery through SteamSpy and public Steam Store endpoints, GOG integration is currently disabled after its public listing endpoint failed compatibility testing. IGDB can enrich the catalog after its private Twitch application credentials are configured in Supabase. Read the data sources and update policy for details and limitations.
